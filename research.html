Projects & Research

Funded engineering work, and the peer-reviewed research behind it

Research Focus

My research sits at the intersection of sustainable engineering, machine learning, and industrial systems: thermal management, circular economy implementation, and manufacturing optimisation. The work has been funded by UKRI, Horizon Europe, the Royal Society, and the Alan Turing Institute, and published with Elsevier, Springer, and IEEE.

The through-line is translating advanced engineering research into practical, commercially viable tools for industrial decarbonisation — not papers that stop at the paper.

Funded Projects

Six projects spanning Horizon Europe, the Royal Society, the Alan Turing Institute, and industry partners including Rolls-Royce and Hitachi Astemo. Each sits at the same intersection: applied engineering research turned into something a manufacturer can actually use.

Enhancing Thermal Management through Machine Learning Algorithms

PhD Research — University of Hertfordshire | Principal Investigator | 2021–2025

Developed a novel framework combining machine learning, bio-inspired design, and Lean manufacturing for advanced thermal management.

Results: 30–70% heat transfer improvement, 60–70% computational reduction, 43% cost reduction, 29% energy savings, and 19% lower emissions.
