Publications

Journal of Cleaner Production 2025

AI-driven diagnostics for energy and material efficiency in SME manufacturing: a techno-economic framework

Harris, M. & Wu, H. (2025). Journal of Cleaner Production, 527, 146731.

Demonstrates a 42.76% reduction in energy costs and 28.95% improvement in material efficiency through AI-enabled process diagnostics applied to SME manufacturing environments.

AI Diagnostics Energy Efficiency SME Manufacturing DOI
Resources, Conservation and Recycling 2024

Circular economy implementation in UK SME manufacturing: barriers, enablers, and policy levers

Harris, M. (2024). Resources, Conservation and Recycling.

Systematic analysis of 120 UK SME manufacturers identifying key barriers to circular economy adoption. Proposes a tiered policy framework aligned with the UK Net Zero Strategy.

Circular Economy Policy UK Manufacturing
Journal of Manufacturing Systems 2023

Machine learning for predictive maintenance in low-data manufacturing environments

Harris, M., Guo, Y. & Williams, P. (2023). Journal of Manufacturing Systems.

Transfer learning approach enabling predictive maintenance models to perform reliably with fewer than 500 training samples. Validated with Rolls-Royce supplier data.

Machine Learning Predictive Maintenance Transfer Learning
Sustainable Production and Consumption 2023

Industrial heat decarbonisation pathways for UK process industries: a techno-economic assessment

Harris, M. & Patel, R. (2023). Sustainable Production and Consumption.

Techno-economic modelling of electrification, hydrogen, and heat pump pathways for ~70% of UK industrial energy demand. Quantifies cost differentials driven by the 4.4× electricity-to-gas price ratio.

Decarbonisation Industrial Heat Net Zero
Applied Energy 2022

Energy efficiency in SME metal forming: process-level measurement and intervention prioritisation

Harris, M. (2022). Applied Energy.

Develops a process-level energy measurement protocol for SME metal forming, demonstrating 18–31% efficiency improvement opportunities invisible to facility-level monitoring. Validated across 12 UK manufacturer sites.

Energy Measurement Metal Forming Industrial SMEs
