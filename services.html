Services

PhD-level engineering expertise applied to real manufacturing problems

Inshira Technologies

AI-enabled manufacturing diagnostics for SMEs

inshira.co.uk

Inshira builds AI diagnostic tools that give SME manufacturers a process-level view of their energy consumption, material waste, and production inefficiency — the information they need to target decarbonisation investment rationally rather than speculatively.

Current focus: UK and European manufacturers. Early pilots in thermal component manufacturing, precision engineering, and food processing.

Consulting & Advisory

Manufacturing Diagnostics

Process-level analysis of energy consumption, material flows, and production inefficiency. Identifies where costs and emissions arise before investment decisions are made.

Decarbonisation Strategy

Evidence-based decarbonisation roadmaps for SME manufacturers — from baseline measurement to prioritised interventions and green finance readiness.

AI for Industrial Systems

Technical advisory on deploying machine learning in manufacturing environments, drawing on applied research with Rolls-Royce and Hitachi Astemo.

Circular Economy Implementation

Material flow mapping, waste identification, and techno-economic modelling of circular economy alternatives for manufacturing processes.
